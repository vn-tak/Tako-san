## Scope

Repair quantity claims, known shopping shortfalls, image fallback and filtered
inventory emptiness. Start the warm/pine/coral typography and vector identity
prototype on inventory and recipe detail. Reuse T02 quantity evidence and keep
legacy ranking type coverage, server cooking/session/revision, tenancy and Week.
Offline cooking must conserve the chosen quantity across compatible lots once.
No payment/auth/production infrastructure or schema change.

## Acceptance

- 2 eggs against 4 required: 100% type match, no-buy false, partial evidence and
  shopping sends 2; contextual packs/bunches remain unresolved without guessing.
- Compatible lots sum; duplicated/conflicting IDs and repeated demands cannot
  create stock. Optional shortages do not invalidate required quantity proof.
- Recipe instructions readable at 16px, desktop overview/preparation columns;
  loading/error inventory does not show invented shortages or enable cooking.
- Existing inventory with a zero-result filter retains stock, labels no results,
  clears filters and returns focus to search. Truly empty inventory offers scan.
- Missing/failed recipe imagery displays a neutral placeholder, preserving valid
  canonical and legacy assets and stopping at placeholder failure.
- Self-hosted fonts include Vietnamese glyphs/license; palette contrast measured.
  Identity is a prototype, not a full finalized brand pack or usability certificate.
- Local browser/mobile/desktop/reduced-motion/keyboard/axe evidence plus repository
  lint/typecheck/tests/migrations/build. Exact results/failures recorded in handoff.
